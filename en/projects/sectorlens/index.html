SectorLens.

From a figure to its evidence: a full-stack platform connecting a company's financial health, industry context, and the sources behind each result.

Verified reproducible casePrivate repository

Read the full context and my contribution

Problem & context

SectorLens connects my Economics background with full-stack development, turning financial and macroeconomic sources into an experience for exploring, comparing, and verifying results. Intel is the first case, with an October 2, 2026 cutoff and seven configured peers. SEC EDGAR supplies financial statements and historical classification; BLS, the Federal Reserve, and BEA provide macro context. The application preserves each universe's scope and differences between fiscal and calendar dates. Results are descriptive; growth decomposition does not establish causality or constitute an investment recommendation.

My contribution

I organized the project around YAML cases, source adapters, calculation functions, and snapshot-based read contracts. I worked on the Python/FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend, the Next.js and TypeScript interface, the source inspector, and reproducible Docker startup. Documented decisions cover decimal precision, missing data, fiscal alignment, lineage, and separating AI from calculations. This individual project connects those decisions to an interface where a person can understand what was calculated and review the evidence.

01

Explore a case at a consistent cutoff

Company, Industry, Compare, Growth, and Methodology preserve the selected analytical run. A person can review trends, indicators, and peer positioning without changing snapshots between views; dates, filters, and guides explain the comparison's scope.

02

Go from a figure to its origin

The inspector exposes the exact value, periods, formula, XBRL tag, SEC document, and dependencies. Tables and charts lead to that evidence while retaining the states and reasons for missing values.

03

Understand context and coverage

Industry analysis distinguishes the strict census, observed XBRL universe, and configured peers. Controls show scope, alignment, and deflator; views retain gaps where information is unavailable. BLS, Federal Reserve, and BEA series provide context with their own units and dates.

01

Precision and traceability from input

Python Decimal and PostgreSQL NUMERIC preserve amounts; the API returns decimal strings, and the interface uses Decimal.js for presentation. Calculation functions retain formulas, revisions, and dependencies. SHA-256 identifies original recordings, and the snapshot pins 257 source and configuration files for offline execution.

02

A reproducible contract between data and interface

FastAPI exposes results and origin through paginated read endpoints; case_id and run_id pin the run in every request. Bootstrap verifies inputs and reuses a consistent persisted run. CI checks offline contracts, types, PostgreSQL integration, builds, and browser journeys; the reviewed local report contains 41 passing integration tests without skips.

03

Optional AI with verified observations

The service checks indicators against PostgreSQL before sending a projection to OpenAI Responses. AI selects and orders templates and IDs; the server renders verified figures, dates, and citations, rejecting unrelated references and extra fields. An explicit flag, key, and model are required. Provider responses were tested in controlled scenarios, without paid calls or validation of a specific remote model.
